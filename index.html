Moving Parts

Big ideas in mathematics, computer science and physics, taken apart so you can watch them work.

Each explainer is a story told through live demos. The figures run the real algorithms and train real models right in your browser, so you can play them, pause them, step through them one move at a time, and change the inputs to see what happens.
